# Engineering Tripos Part IIA

Repository for labs and coursework for Engineering Tripos Part IIA

## Latex Workshop

We use [VSCode Latex Workshop](https://github.com/James-Yu/LaTeX-Workshop/wiki) to edit our latex documents. Please install this in VSCode as a drop-in local replacement for Overleaf.

Please keep the `.vscode/settings.json` file as this lays out how to build and clean latex properly.

- To build, simply *save* the latex file
- All build artefacts should be cleaned automatically, keeping only the pdf file in the relevant directory
- It may takes 2/3 seconds to compile and clean, so please be patient

## Templates

We use some templates for styling our FTRs. The `cls` file is available in the top level `templates` folder for reustability. ## Coursework Details

### Lab Reports

Students must provide their own lab books and paper for recording/plotting data. It is best practice to plot graphs while at the bench to verify data points. **All original readings must be retained and submitted.**

* **Time allocation:** Writing the report should take no more than 2–3 hours after the lab.
* **Length:** A total length of 3–5 pages is standard.
* **Format:** Word-processed documents with properly typeset equations are recommended.
* **Precedence:** Instructions from Lab Leaders take precedence over departmental guidelines.

### Feedback Sheets

All reports (or essays for Group E) must be submitted with a **coversheet**.
* **Criteria:** Markers look for a clear record of practical work and appropriate discussion.
* **Collaboration:** Readings taken jointly may be shared, but reports must be written **individually**.

### Full Technical Reports (FTRs)

FTRs are designed to enhance technical communication skills. Each requires ~10 hours of work.
* **Timeline:** Complete experimental work early. Submit the standard lab report no later than **Week 6**.
* **Registration:** Register your choice of FTR by the end of the term in which the module takes place.

**Structure & Submission:**
* **Length:** Maximum 10 pages (excluding original lab report appendix).
* **Formatting:** Minimum 11pt font, typed/word-processed to professional standard.
* **Content:** Extensive analysis and discussion of technical literature. The marked module lab report should be included as an appendix.

**FTR Deadlines:**
* **Michaelmas Term FTR:** 4pm on Wednesday of Week 9.
* **Lent Term FTR:** 4pm on Wednesday of Week 9.
* *Submission via Moodle with coversheet.*

### Marking

* **Submission Deadline:** Completed reports must be submitted within **15 days** of the experiment (inclusive of lab date).
    * *Example:* If lab is Wednesday, deadline is 4pm on the Wednesday the week after next.
* **Return Deadline:** Marked reports returned within 15 term days.
* **Planning:** Any experiment intended for an FTR should be done early enough for the marked report to be returned before the FTR deadline.
